What additional evidence is needed?

Verified physical inventory and observations of queues, arrivals, or turned-away drivers would support further investigation. They would not, by themselves, prove that a fee or another intervention is effective.

Read the boundary

  • Port capacity is inferred, not verified inventory; the spread is assumption sensitivity, not a confidence interval.
  • At an inferred single-port station, every idle minute is full-occupancy idle by definition.
  • No queue, arrival, or turned-away-driver data is present.
  • Charging is assumed to begin at session start; reconstructed intervals are not measured charging profiles.
