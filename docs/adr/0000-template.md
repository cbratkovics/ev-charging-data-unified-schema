# ADR-0000 — Title (YYYY-MM-DD)

**Context.** What forced a decision: the constraint, the data, the incident. Cite paths and numbers.

**Decision.** What was decided, precisely enough that a reader could reproduce it. Name the
alternatives that were considered and why they lost.

**Consequences.** What changes for the code, the data, the owner's routine; what is now harder;
what must be revisited and when.

---
Conventions: one file per decision, numbered globally (`docs/adr/NNNN-slug.md`), newest number
wins; departures from a brief are ADRs too; thresholds and tolerances always come with the
measurement that produced them, or the words "not calibrated".
